Stellar Evolution Timeline

Protostar

A young star still forming from collapsing gas and dust clouds.

Main Sequence

The longest phase where stars fuse hydrogen into helium in their cores.

Red Giant

Stars expand and cool as they exhaust their hydrogen fuel.

White Dwarf

The dense, hot core left behind after a star sheds its outer layers.

Supernova

The explosive death of massive stars, creating heavy elements.
