Deep Space Objects (DSO) Note:

These objects are so massive and spread out that gravity becomes extremely weak at their "surface." The tiny weights you see reflect the enormous distances from their centers of mass. This is why galaxies don't collapse under their own gravity!

Quantum Scale Objects:

These are individual atoms. Gravity is practically nonexistent at this scale, so expect microscopic weights far smaller than anything you can feel.
