The Cosmic Leviathan

Type: Unknown Magnitude: −∞ Distance: 32.65 B ly

The Cosmic Leviathan is an incomprehensible entity lurking just beyond the Prismatic Helix, concealed within its shifting chromatic veil. Its magnitude is immeasurable — instruments simply return infinity. No known physics can account for its existence. Ancient astronomical texts, dismissed as myth, describe a creature woven from collapsed dimensions and dead light, older than the universe itself. It does not move. It does not need to. It has always been there, waiting behind the colors, patient beyond the concept of time.
