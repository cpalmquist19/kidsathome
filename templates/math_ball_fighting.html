Fictional numbers: names from the Fictional Googology Wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0). They are made up, not real maths: in this game they all beat Infinity, and between themselves the bigger one wins, using the wiki's own lists of which numbers are bigger and smaller. A few names were left out to keep the game kid-friendly.
