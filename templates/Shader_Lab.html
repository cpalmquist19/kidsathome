What you’re seeing

Light curves around an absence.

The hole is a dark mask. Around it, the shader twists coordinates before sampling a procedural starfield, which creates the illusion of gravitational lensing. The disk is layered noise bent into polar coordinates—not a simulation, but a fast visual model.
